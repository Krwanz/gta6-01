# Solmar Bay

A make.fun entry for gta6-01: an original 2D top-down open-world crime game that runs in the browser. You play Rico Vale, a courier who owes $4,000 to a loan shark, and you work the debt down across five story missions set around the bay city of Solmar.

The city is yours to roam on foot or by car: streets full of traffic and pedestrians, cars you can take, police who chase you when your wanted level climbs, and fights on foot when a job turns rough. A HUD keeps your stars, cash, health and objective in view, with a live minimap showing the streets, your position and the mission marker. There is a start screen, pause, and your progress (cash, missions done, position) is saved in localStorage so you can pick up where you left off.

## How to run

Open `game/index.html` in a browser, or serve the `game/` folder with any static server. The `main.ts` script builds the entry dossier (game files plus screenshots) for the competition.

No libraries, no network requests — everything is plain Canvas 2D and synthesized WebAudio.
